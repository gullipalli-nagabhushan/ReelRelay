Plain answers

Questions people actually ask

No jargon. If something here is unclear, the README has the same answers with more detail.

ReelRelay is a small app that runs on your own computer and saves videos or audio from YouTube, Instagram and Facebook when you paste a link. It works in your browser, but the program itself lives on your machine.

Double-click the ReelRelay shortcut on your desktop. A black window opens, the app starts, and your browser shows the page automatically. Keep the black window open while you use the app; closing it stops ReelRelay. If you do not have the shortcut yet, run create-desktop-shortcut.bat once.

No. ReelRelay has no sign-up, no login, no payment and no ads. Everything runs locally and free. You only need an account of your own if you want to download a private, age-restricted or members-only post — see the question about sign-in below.

Up to {{ max_height_label }} (4320p) when the original video offers it. ReelRelay lists every quality the source actually provides, so a 1080p upload will not magically become 8K. High resolutions need ffmpeg to combine video and audio — the app tells you on the preview page if ffmpeg is missing.

Wherever your browser saves downloads. That is usually your Downloads folder. ReelRelay itself does not keep a copy, so nothing piles up inside the project folder. You can change the destination in your browser settings.

Some posts are private, age-gated or members-only, and the platform will not hand them out to an anonymous visitor. If it is your account, you can export a cookies.txt file from your browser and point YTDLP_COOKIES_FILE at it in .env. That file is a login token: keep it on your own computer, never share it, and delete it when you are done. If the post belongs to someone else and is private, there is no legitimate way around it.

Yes. Choose MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV or the untouched original stream. ReelRelay fills in title, artist, album and year where the source provides them, and attaches the thumbnail as cover art. MP3 and FLAC give the most reliable artwork.

No. There is no analytics, no account system and no cloud storage. The app talks directly to YouTube, Instagram or Facebook from your computer, and the file goes from them to your browser. ReelRelay keeps media only in memory while a transfer is running.

Install ffmpeg once, then restart ReelRelay. On Windows the easiest way is winget install Gyan.FFmpeg. Without ffmpeg you can still download single-file video qualities and the original audio stream, but 4K/8K merging and MP3/FLAC/M4A/WAV conversion stay disabled.

It depends on where you live and what you download. Download only content you own, that is offered under a permissive licence, or that you have permission to save. Respect copyright law and each platform's terms. ReelRelay is a personal tool; you are responsible for how you use it.
